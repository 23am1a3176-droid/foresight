# Data Quality & Cleaning Report

Project FORESIGHT — NorthBay Living

## Sources ingested
- `sales_daily.csv`: 36,550 rows, 50 SKUs, 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31
- `sku_master.csv`: 50 rows
- `calendar.csv`: 731 rows, 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31
- `inventory_snapshots.csv`: 4,800 rows, 200 SKUs, monthly snapshots

## Key finding: SKU coverage mismatch
`inventory_snapshots.csv` contains **200 SKUs**, but only **50 of them** (SKU001, SKU002, SKU003, ...) have a matching record in `sku_master.csv` and sales history in `sales_daily.csv`. The other **150 SKUs** (SKU051–SKU200) appear only in the inventory extract, with no product master data and no sales history to forecast from.

**Decision:** the forecasting and risk-scoring pipeline is scoped to the 50 SKUs with complete data (matches the client's description of their active catalogue). The orphan inventory rows are preserved in `excluded_skus.csv` rather than silently dropped, and flagged for the client to confirm whether these are a system export error, a discontinued-product list, or SKUs not yet launched for sale.

## Other data-quality checks
- **Revenue consistency:** `Units_Sold × Price` matches the `Revenue` column exactly (0 mismatches found out of 36,550 rows). Kept as an explicit reconciliation check rather than assumed.
- **Duplicates:** no duplicate (date, SKU) rows found in sales; no duplicate SKUs in master; no duplicate (snapshot_date, SKU) rows in inventory.
- **Missing values:** `calendar.holiday` and `calendar.promotion_event` are blank on non-holiday/non-promo days by design — filled with the literal `"None"` rather than treated as missing data.
- **Date coverage:** all 50 in-scope SKUs have a complete daily record for every day in the 2024-01-01–2025-12-31 window (no gaps).
- **Zero-sales days:** average 0.55% of days per SKU have zero units sold — low, and consistent with occasional stockouts or genuinely slow days rather than a data gap.
- **Negative gross margin:** 16 SKUs are priced below cost (SKU002, SKU007, SKU009, SKU010, SKU011, SKU016, SKU018, SKU020, SKU023, SKU024, SKU028, SKU033, SKU037, SKU038, SKU040, SKU048). Not treated as an error — flagged as a pricing/margin issue worth raising with the client separately from the forecasting work.
- **Inventory cadence:** inventory snapshots are monthly (1st of each month), while sales are daily. The risk-scoring step will need to reconcile this by treating the most recent snapshot as the current inventory position at forecast time, not by resampling to daily.

## Reproducibility
This report and the processed datasets are regenerated by running `python src/pipeline.py` from the project root. No manual spreadsheet edits are used anywhere in this pipeline.
